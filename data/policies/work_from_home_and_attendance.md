# Work From Home and Attendance Policy

Sample policy for Acme Corp (fictional).

## Working Hours

The standard work week is Monday to Friday, 40 hours per week. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 local time, during which employees are expected to be available. Outside core hours, employees may plan their schedule flexibly with their team.

## Attendance

Employees record attendance by logging in to the HR portal on each working day. Missing attendance entries must be regularised within 5 working days; unregularised days are treated as leave without pay after manager review.

Arriving after 11:00 more than 3 times in a month without informing the manager will be discussed with the reporting manager.

## Hybrid Work

Employees follow a hybrid model: a minimum of 3 days per week in the office, and up to 2 days per week working from home. Teams may agree on common office days.

## Work From Home Requests

Additional work-from-home days beyond the standard 2 days per week require manager approval at least 1 working day in advance. Extended remote work (more than 2 consecutive weeks) needs approval from the department head and HR.

Employees on probation may work from home only with explicit manager approval.

While working from home, employees must be reachable during core hours, use the company VPN, and keep confidential information secure. Working from a location outside the country requires prior approval from HR due to tax and data-protection rules.

## Overtime and Compensatory Off

Employees do not receive paid overtime. Work on weekends or holidays at the manager's request earns a compensatory off day that must be used within 60 days.
