# Code of Conduct

Sample policy for Acme Corp (fictional).

## Respectful Workplace

Acme Corp is committed to a workplace free from harassment and discrimination. Discrimination based on race, religion, caste, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, marital status or any other protected characteristic is prohibited.

## Prevention of Sexual Harassment

Sexual harassment in any form is not tolerated. Complaints can be raised with the Internal Committee (IC) in writing within 3 months of the incident. The IC completes its inquiry within 90 days. Retaliation against anyone who raises a complaint in good faith is a disciplinary offence.

## Confidentiality and Data Protection

Employees must protect confidential company, customer and employee information. Personal or health data must only be accessed on a need-to-know basis and must never be shared over personal email or messaging apps. Suspected data breaches must be reported to the security team within 24 hours.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any outside employment, business interest or relationship that could conflict with their duties. Accepting gifts worth more than INR 2,000 from vendors or customers is not allowed.

## Raising Concerns

Employees can raise concerns with their manager, HR, or anonymously through the whistle-blower channel. All reports are handled confidentially.

## Disciplinary Process

Violations of this code may lead to disciplinary action, from a written warning up to termination, depending on severity. Employees will be given an opportunity to present their side before any decision is made.
