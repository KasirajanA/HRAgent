# Leave Policy

Sample policy for Acme Corp (fictional). Effective 1 January 2026. Applies to all full-time and contract employees; interns are covered in the section "Interns".

## General Rules

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Leave is counted in working days: weekends (Saturday and Sunday) and company holidays falling inside a leave period are not counted.

All leave must be applied for through the HR portal or the HR chat assistant and approved by the reporting manager. A request remains "pending" until the manager approves or rejects it. Days in pending requests are blocked from the available balance.

A single leave request cannot span two leave years. If your leave crosses 31 December, submit two separate requests.

If an employee takes leave without sufficient balance, the shortfall is treated as Loss of Pay (LOP). LOP requires prior approval from the reporting manager and is deducted from the monthly payroll.

## Earned Leave (EL)

Employees earn 18 days of Earned Leave per year. EL accrues at 1.5 days per month and is credited on the 1st of the following month. An employee earns the monthly credit only if they were on the company's rolls for at least 15 days of that month.

EL cannot be availed during the probation period, but it continues to accrue and becomes usable once probation is completed.

EL requests longer than 2 working days must be applied at least 7 calendar days in advance. A single EL request may not exceed 15 working days. Longer breaks require approval from the department head and HR.

### Carry Forward and Lapse

Up to 10 days of unused EL can be carried forward to the next leave year. Any EL above 10 days at the end of the year lapses on 31 December. EL cannot be encashed while in service.

## Casual Leave (CL)

Employees receive 6 days of Casual Leave per year, credited in full on 1 January. Employees who join during the year receive a prorated CL credit based on the months remaining in the year (the joining month counts if the employee joins on or before the 15th). Prorated values are rounded to the nearest half day.

CL is meant for short, unplanned personal needs. It can be taken as a half day. A maximum of 2 consecutive working days of CL is allowed per request. CL can be used during probation. Unused CL lapses at the end of the year and is not carried forward.

## Sick Leave (SL)

Employees receive 12 days of Sick Leave per year, credited in full on 1 January and prorated for mid-year joiners in the same way as CL.

SL can be taken as a half day and can be used during probation. Because illness is unplanned, SL can be applied retroactively for up to 7 days after the leave started.

A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive working days. Unused SL lapses at the end of the year.

## Parental Leave (Secondary Caregiver)

A secondary caregiver (for example, a parent who is not the birthing or primary adoptive parent) is entitled to 10 working days of Parental Leave per birth or adoption. It must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.

To be eligible, the employee must have completed 6 months of continuous service on the start date of the leave. Parental Leave must be applied at least 14 days in advance where possible.

Maternity leave and primary-caregiver adoption leave are governed by statutory requirements (26 weeks) and are processed directly by HR, not through self-service. Please contact HR at least 8 weeks before the expected date.

## Bereavement Leave (BL)

Employees may take up to 3 working days of Bereavement Leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, parent-in-law, grandparent). BL can be applied retroactively for up to 7 days and does not require advance notice.

## Interns

Interns are entitled to 3 days of Casual Leave and 6 days of Sick Leave per year, prorated for the internship joining month. Interns are not eligible for Earned Leave, Parental Leave or Bereavement Leave under this policy; any exceptional leave is at the discretion of the manager and HR.

## Leave During Notice Period

Employees serving their notice period may use available SL and CL. EL during the notice period requires manager approval and may extend the last working day. Unused EL is settled as per the full-and-final settlement rules.

## Cancelling Leave

A pending request can be cancelled by the employee at any time. An approved request can be cancelled before the leave starts; the days are returned to the balance. Contact HR to modify leave that has already started.
