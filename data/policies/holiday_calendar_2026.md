## Optional Holidays

In addition to the list above, every employee may take 2 optional (restricted) holidays per year for festivals of their choice. Optional holidays must be informed to the manager at least 3 days in advance and cannot be carried forward.

## Working on a Holiday

Employees asked by their manager to work on a company holiday receive one compensatory off day, which must be used within 60 days.
